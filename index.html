Farm Inputs & Where To Find Them

Maize Inputs
  • Certified Seed: Kenya Seed, Pioneer
  • Fertilizer: DAP, Urea, NPK
  • Herbicides: 2,4D, Lumax Gold
Beans Inputs
  • Clean seed: Rosecoco, Mwitemania
  • Rhizobium: NoduMax, Biofix
  • NPK fertilizer or manure
Potato Inputs
  • Clean seed tubers: KEPHIS certified
  • Fungicides: Ridomil Gold, Dithane M-45
  • Blended fertilizer NPK

Find these inputs at agro-dealers near you, or buy from trusted vendors below. Always look for KEPHIS certification on seeds!
